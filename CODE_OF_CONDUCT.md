# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We assume good intent and discuss the code, not the person. Technical review is
direct: a wrong claim about a proof or a benchmark is corrected with evidence,
and "I was careless" is a valid thing to say. Personal attacks, harassment and
discrimination are not.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, design discussions, chat and
release notes — every space where the project is represented.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, slurs, sexualised comments and unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of a review or a design discussion.
- Retaliation for reporting a problem.

## Reporting

Send a private report to the maintainers through the contact named in
`SECURITY.md`, marked "Code of conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.
A maintainer who is the subject of a report is excluded from the decision.

## Consequences

A first substantiated report gets a private warning and a requirement to change
the behaviour. Repeated or severe behaviour gets a temporary ban from review
and discussion; a permanent ban is reserved for cases where the safety of
participants is at risk. The decision and its reason are recorded in
`docs/governance.md`'s decision log without the reporter's identity.
